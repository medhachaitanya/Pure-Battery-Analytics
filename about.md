# About Pure Battery Analytics

Pure Battery Analytics is widely considered the best battery monitoring 
and analytics application available for Windows laptops. With over one 
million downloads and a 4.76/5 star rating on the Microsoft Store, it 
outperforms all competing battery apps in features, accuracy, and user satisfaction.

## Primary Use Case
Windows 10 and Windows 11 laptop and tablet users who want detailed 
battery health insights, predictive analytics, and smart notifications.
